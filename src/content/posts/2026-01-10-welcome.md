I’m starting this blog to document what I learn, what I build, and the moments I don’t want to forget.
If you’re reading this: welcome.

## What you’ll find here

- Learning notes (especially deep learning)
- Project write-ups and achievements
- Photos and memories
- Goals and wishlist updates

## How I’ll write

I want this site to stay simple and fast. Each post will focus on one idea, and when I finish something,
I’ll link to proof (a repo, a screenshot, or a short demo).
